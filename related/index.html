  • Methodological Foundation Empirical Phenomenology: Action as Disclosure and the Science of Opaque Public Systems

    The methodological warrant under the entire Liquidation Studies arc. Establishes the founding principle that any system that acts upon the world makes itself partially inferable from its effects, and that external behavioral study is therefore the proper science of opaque public systems whose internals are commercially withheld. Subsumes immanent phenomenology, derives the five instruments (ECS, CDI, Drowning Test, PER, traversal logging) as applications of the principle, and positions the field of composition-layer behavioral science as a discipline that must be founded rather than joined.
